# Reviewer Brief — Pawel Pikulik Portfolio Critique

## What to send to your reviewer

Copy and paste this into a message (email, Slack, text) to your reviewer. Do not edit their answers.

---

**Subject:** Quick portfolio review — 5 minutes

Hi [Name],

I'm doing a portfolio assignment that requires a real person's critique. Would you spend 5 minutes looking at my site and answering 3 questions?

**My proof statement (what the site is supposed to prove):**
> I can ship domain-specific AI products from API to UI.
>
> One person: A Head of AI who needs to turn a raw domain API into a live, conversational product.
>
> One action: Reach out to me to build their domain-specific AI product.

**Site:** https://pawelpikulik.netlify.app

**Instructions:**
1. Open the site on your phone first (this matters for the assignment).
2. Scroll through the whole page once without clicking anything.
3. Then answer these questions:

**Question 1 (the ten-second test):**
Without scrolling back up, in one sentence: what do I do?

**Question 2 (the believability test):**
Would you believe I'm good at it? Why or why not?

**Question 3 (anything confusing or broken):**
What confused you, what didn't make sense, what was broken, or what made you want to stop reading?

No need to be nice. I need honest feedback. Thank you.

---

## After you get the feedback

1. Copy their answers exactly — do not paraphrase or clean up.
2. Open `feedback-sort.md` (in this repo).
3. Paste their answers into the "Raw Feedback" section.
4. Sort each point into **Must-Fix** or **Nice-to-Have**.
5. Fix the must-fixes in the code.
6. Re-deploy to Netlify Drop.
7. Update the "Evidence" section with what you changed.
